ITEL Foundation

We are proud to announce that Datamatrix.AI has been selected for incubation at the Immersive Technology and Entrepreneurship Labs (ITEL) Foundation — a non-profit organization established with a bold and defining mission: to make India a global technology leader in select areas.

ITEL operates on a powerful premise — that India's unique challenges of high population density and low household incomes, long considered unsolvable, are precisely the problems that can propel India to the forefront of global technology innovation. By building and deploying solutions at scale domestically and extending them to nations facing similar challenges, ITEL aims to position India as an exporter of technology leadership, not just an importer.

Being selected as an ITEL incubatee is a validation of Datamatrix.AI's vision and our commitment to building AI products that address real-world, complex problems. We look forward to contributing to this national mission and to building AI solutions that matter — for India and for the world.

Software Technology Parks of India
Datamatrix.AI is a registered startup under the Software Technology Parks of India (STPI), an autonomous body under the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, Government of India. STPI supports India's technology sector through infrastructure, policy, and ecosystem development — and recognises Datamatrix.AI as part of its cohort of enterprise AI innovators driving India's digital transformation.

BharatNetra FinTech Centre of Excellence
Datamatrix.AI Pvt. Ltd. has been selected for the prestigious BharatNetra FinTech Centre of Excellence accelerator program at the Odisha Technology Centre, Bhubaneswar. As one of the country's emerging innovation hubs for FinTech and InsurTech, the CoE provides strategic mentorship, structured incubation, and pathways to market readiness and global opportunities.
