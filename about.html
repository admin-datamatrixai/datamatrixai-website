About Us

Datamatrix.AI is an India based enterprise AI product company incorporated in April 2025, headquartered in Bangalore, and founded by acclaimed industry professionals with over 28 years of experience across Power DISCOM, BFSI, Healthcare, Ecommerce and Education. We build responsible, scalable and innovative AI products for real world enterprise environments, with multiple AI products already implemented and delivering value for clients in India. We are proud to be incubated at the ITEL Foundation in Chennai, a national body committed to making India a global technology leader, and are part of the Bharatnetra Accelerator Program for BFSI, a joint venture between GFTN Singapore and the Government of India — placing Datamatrix.AI on the global map from inception.
