Strategic Advisor

We are pleased to welcome Dr. Sanjay Mohapatra as Strategic Advisor to DatamatrixAI.

Dr. Sanjay brings senior leadership experience from two of the Big 4 consulting firms, having managed operations across three continents. He also spent over fifteen years in academia as Professor at Xavier Institute of Management, Bhubaneswar, and currently serves as Domain Advisor for Emerging Technology and Artificial Intelligence with the Government of Odisha, along with visiting faculty positions at IIT Guwahati and IIM Sambalpur.

We look forward to leveraging his deep understanding of IT strategy, innovation, and enterprise technology adoption to strengthen key strategic areas including customer acquisition, sales strategy, and partnership development, as we continue building AI products that help organizations gain a competitive edge, strengthen their brand, and become more productive, efficient, and agile through the responsible adoption of AI.

We look forward to the value his guidance will bring to our growth journey ahead.
